Documented Structural Vulnerabilities

Weak contracts with no auditing + single ESP per location + high visa volume + high success rate variance = conditions where abuse (appointment scarcity, fee manipulation, service quality) is more likely.

Key Risk Combinations:
• High ESP reliance + low supervision visibility
• Single-provider markets + high volume
• Fee opacity + optional services promotion
• Contracts lacking performance standards
• High-volume locations (China, Türkiye, India) with concentrated ESP use

F. Options and Recommendations

Synthesizes findings from A–E into actionable policy solutions. Ranges from modest contract improvements to ambitious EU-level coordination. Recommendations address identified risks and misaligned incentives.

Policy Intervention Pathways

Different levels of intervention matched to risk severity and implementation feasibility. Some changes can happen quickly via contracts; others require legislative or institutional coordination.

Quick Wins (Contract Level): Mandatory appointment availability standards, fee transparency requirements, performance auditing clauses

Medium-term (MS Coordination): Benchmark contracts across Member States, standardize ESP monitoring protocols, real-time data sharing

Structural (EU Level): Harmonized procurement rules for ESPs, EU-wide supervision framework, digital visa architecture limiting outsourcing scope

Recommendation Framework

Solutions are most effective in combination: stronger contracts alone won't work without monitoring; monitoring won't work without clear legal standards; all require consistent enforcement.
